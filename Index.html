Delivery

Shipping & Returns

Shipping

Orders ship within 1–2 business days. Standard delivery takes 3–6 business days across India; metro pincodes often see next-day delivery. Shipping is free on orders over ₹4,999 — a flat ₹149 applies below that.

Returns

Not the right fit? You have 14 days from delivery to send it back for a full refund, provided tags are intact and the item is unworn. Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days of us receiving the return.

Exchanges

Need a different size or color? Start a return and place a new order — this is the fastest way to get the right piece to you without waiting on inventory holds.
